Mill Valley Storyteller

Ask a question about Mill Valley history, or ask for a story. Storyteller reads four collections (the 2AM Club, Mill Valley baseball, the Mill Valley Tennis Club and Marin golf) with their published histories, the sources behind their footnotes and thousands of digitized local newspaper articles, then writes a short account with numbered citations: a starting point for your own research.
